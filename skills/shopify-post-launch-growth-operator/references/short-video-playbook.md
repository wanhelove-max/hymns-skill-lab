# Short-Video And UGC Playbook

## Script Structure

Use 15-35 seconds unless the platform/content idea needs more.

1. Hook in first 1-2 seconds.
2. Show the product or styling result immediately.
3. Explain one reason to care.
4. Show material/price/care truth without overclaiming.
5. End with one route: collection, PDP, material guide, or email signup.

## Hook Types

- "I wanted the gold look without paying solid-gold prices."
- "This is not solid gold, and that is the point."
- "Three ways to wear one gold-tone chain."
- "What I check before buying gold-plated jewelry."
- "A simple chain that makes a plain tee look intentional."
- "Gift jewelry without guessing someone’s fine-jewelry budget."

## Video Formats

| Format | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Outfit before/after | Awareness and styling | Show same outfit with and without jewelry. |
| Macro material shot | Brand trust | Close-up shine, clasp, links, texture. |
| 3-style challenge | Consideration | One product, work/weekend/night. |
| Material explainer | Search and trust | Explain gold-tone/gold-plated/not solid gold. |
| Gift guide | Seasonal conversion | Route to gift collection or category. |
| Care routine | Retention and trust | Route to Material & Care. |
| Creator try-on | Social proof when real | Disclose incentives and do not script fake praise. |

## Caption Formula

- One concrete benefit.
- One material truth.
- One CTA.

Example:

`Gold-look styling without solid-gold pricing. Check the product page for finish, base material, and care notes. Shop gold-tone chains.`

## Landing Route Map

- Styling video -> relevant collection.
- Material explainer -> Material & Care page + collection.
- Gift guide -> gift/category collection.
- Product demo -> PDP.
- Care routine -> Material & Care + post-purchase email.

## Compliance

Do not say:

- real gold
- waterproof
- tarnish-proof
- hypoallergenic
- never fades
- luxury quality
- customer favorite
- viral
- sold out repeatedly
- limited stock

Unless the claim is verifiable and documented.
