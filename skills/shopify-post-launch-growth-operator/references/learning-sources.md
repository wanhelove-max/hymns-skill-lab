# Continuous Learning System

Use this reference when the user wants the growth skill to keep improving from social platforms, communities, creators, and campaign results.

## Learning Principle

Never freeze the growth playbook. Social platforms change quickly. Treat every outside tactic as a hypothesis until it is tested against the store's product, audience, landing page, and compliance rules.

## Source Tiers

1. Official sources
   - TikTok Ads Help and Creative Center.
   - Meta Business / Ads Library.
   - YouTube / Google Ads Shorts guidance.
   - Shopify social commerce and channel documentation.
   - FTC/platform ad policies.

2. Operator communities
   - Reddit ecommerce and ad communities.
   - Shopify seller communities.
   - UGC creator communities.
   - Founder/operator newsletters and posts.

3. Creative observation
   - TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest, Meta Ad Library.
   - Competitor and adjacent category brands.
   - Comments/questions under viral product videos.

4. Own data
   - Shopify analytics.
   - GA4 and Search Console.
   - Email metrics.
   - Platform ad metrics.
   - Review/support questions.

## Evidence Labels

Label every learning:

- `official`: platform or regulator source.
- `operator-anecdote`: practitioner claim or community post.
- `case-study`: source provides concrete result and context.
- `creative-observation`: pattern seen in live content/ad libraries.
- `own-data`: measured in the store's analytics.

Confidence:

- `high`: official rule or repeated own-data result.
- `medium`: multiple independent operator examples or well-documented case study.
- `low`: one anecdote, one creator claim, or unverified agency post.

## Weekly Workflow

1. Collect 10-20 observations.
2. Group them by pattern: hook, first frame, format, offer, landing route, proof point, editing, comment insight.
3. Create 5 testable angles.
4. Produce 10-15 hooks and 3-5 scripts.
5. Match every script to a landing route.
6. Publish/test.
7. Record results and kill/iterate/scale.

## Common 2026 Patterns To Test

- Fast hook and visible product in the opening seconds.
- Creator-style, native-feeling vertical video.
- One message per creative.
- Material/trust proof tied to the landing page.
- Multiple hook variants for the same video body.
- Refresh creative before fatigue.
- Use comments and support questions as script inputs.
- Treat landing page as an extension of the ad, not a separate asset.

## Compliance Filters

Reject any tactic that depends on:

- fake UGC or fake customer quotes
- fake review counts
- fake low stock or countdowns
- false discount anchors
- unsupported material/performance claims
- undisclosed paid creator endorsement
- platform policy workarounds
