# Post-Launch Growth Workflow

## Funnel

1. Awareness
   - organic TikTok/Reels/Shorts
   - creator videos
   - Pinterest/Instagram posts
   - SEO snippets and journal distribution

2. Consideration
   - material education
   - styling guides
   - gift guides
   - product comparison content

3. Conversion
   - collection/PDP route matching
   - clear material and care proof
   - shipping/returns/support confidence
   - review collection when real reviews exist

4. Retention
   - post-purchase care guide
   - review request
   - second-purchase pairing
   - winback

## Content Pillars For Gold-Tone Jewelry

- Styling transformations: one accessory changes the outfit.
- Price/value: gold look without solid-gold cost.
- Material honesty: what gold-tone/gold-plated means.
- Care: how to store and clean pieces.
- Gift selection: choosing size/style without guessing.
- Men’s jewelry: chains, rings, bracelets, proportion.

## Metrics

Track:

- 3-second hold rate / hook retention
- average watch time
- profile click rate
- landing page CTR
- product view rate
- add-to-cart rate
- checkout started
- conversion rate
- CPA / ROAS when paid
- email signup rate
- review collection rate

## Operating Rhythm

Weekly:

- publish content batch
- check top hooks
- identify winning angle
- route winner to matching landing page
- create two iterations of the winner
- update growth log

Monthly:

- refresh content pillars
- review product/category performance
- update landing page recommendations
- decide whether to add apps: wishlist, Klaviyo/Omnisend, loyalty, upsell
- review `content/growth-learning/` and promote proven lessons into the next plan
